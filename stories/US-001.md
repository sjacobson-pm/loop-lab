# US-001 — CeilingTo rounds a decimal up to the next multiple

## Intent

Downstream reports need a single, shared way to round a decimal up to an
arbitrary multiple. This story delivers `CeilingTo` and nothing else.

## Criteria

Cited by anchor. Do not restate.

- `spec/rounding.md#ceiling-to-basic`
- `spec/rounding.md#ceiling-to-negative`
- `spec/rounding.md#ceiling-to-invalid-multiple`
- `spec/rounding.md#ceiling-to-scale`

## Acceptance criteria

The one place criteria text may appear, because these describe the shape of
the delivered artifact rather than the behaviour under test.

- A public static method `CeilingTo(decimal value, decimal multiple)` exists
  in `src/Rounding.cs`, namespace `LoopLab.Rounding`.
- Unit tests live in `tests/unit/RoundingTests.cs`.
- Every criterion above has at least one test that fails against the base
  commit and passes after implementation.

## Out of scope

- `FloorTo`, `RoundTo`, banker's rounding.
- Any `double` overload.
- Any change to files outside `src/` and `tests/unit/`.

## Notes

`PROJECT.md` is advisory context only and may not be cited as a criterion.
